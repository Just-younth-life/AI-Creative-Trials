How to Play
  • Place exactly one Westie in every row and every column.
  • One Westie per colored region. They never touch — not even diagonally.
  • Minesweeper-style elimination：After each placement, its row, column, region and neighbors grey out as blocked.
  • Limited moves：Tap a placed Westie to pick it up again. Moves scale with difficulty (2 on 4×4, up to 4 on 8×8).
  • Limited restarts：↻ Restarts are capped per level (3 on small boards, 1 on large) and refill the next day.
  • A wrong tap costs 1 bone and leaves a permanent red X you can review in the mistake log.
Why 32 Levels?

Following the original non-linear size curve (4→5→6→7→8→9→10×10) and the rhythm of "variants every 5 levels, hand-tuned milestones", the 32 levels are split into 5 stages: the grid rises smoothly from 4×4 to 8×8， with three dials — region shapes, preset hints and bone supply. I deliberately stopped at 32 instead of 100 so every level can be verified as uniquely solvable with a controllable curve — quality over quantity. The daily puzzle is a fixed 9×9 arena for veterans.
